Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Accessrank, Inc. ("Accessrank", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit our website, create an account, or use our services. We aim to collect only what we need to run our services well.

01What we collect

  • Information you provide. Name, work email, company, store URL, and message content when you request an audit, contact sales, or create an account.
  • Service data. Content and code from websites you connect to Accessrank so we can audit and remediate them.
  • Usage data. Log data, device and browser information, and interactions with our website and dashboard.
  • Billing data. Billing contact details and limited payment metadata; card details are processed by our payment provider and are not stored by us.

02How we use it

We use the information to provide and improve the services, run audits and remediation, communicate with you, process payments, provide support, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

03Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we process personal data on the bases of performance of a contract, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the services, your consent (where requested), and compliance with legal obligations.

04Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember preferences, and limited analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect functionality.

05Sharing

We share information with service providers who process it on our behalf (such as hosting, analytics, email, and payment providers), each under obligations to protect it. We may disclose information if required by law, to enforce our agreements, or in connection with a merger or acquisition. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

06Retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the services, then for a reasonable period to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You can request deletion as described below.

07Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of California and the EEA/UK have specific rights under the CCPA/CPRA and GDPR. To exercise any right, contact us at info@accessrank.ai. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

09Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to info@accessrank.ai. If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you by email or through the service.
